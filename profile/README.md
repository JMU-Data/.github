## How these resources are organized

- **One course = one public repository and one published course site.**
- Course repositories contain public teaching materials, labs, examples, and reusable learning resources.
- Private student data, submissions, grades, credentials, and internal Canvas operations are kept outside the public repositories.

## Maintainer

These resources are maintained by **[Dr. Xuebin Wei](https://www.jmu.edu/cise/people/faculty/wei-xuebin.shtml)**, Associate Professor in the **[School of Integrated Sciences](https://www.jmu.edu/cise/depts/sis/index.shtml)** at James Madison University.

His teaching and research focus on data science, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, GIS/geospatial analysis, and social data analytics.
